**Three findings from the research that change the plan:**

1. **Drop the tea, powder and supplement idea.** In Morocco, food supplements must be authorized by AMMPS or declared to ONSSA, and the press reports they may only be sold through pharmacies and parapharmacies. Tea packers and distributors need ONSSA sanitary authorization (Decree 2-13-711, Art. 7). An imported "herbal blend" brand is a legal and seizure risk you cannot afford on $500.
2. **None of these gadgets is unknown in Morocco.** Decompression belts sell for 369–689 MAD (Saneo, Jumia, Oknive, Miso). Vibrating posture trainers sell for 49–199 MAD, neck traction pillows for 95–189 MAD, and smart cupping devices for 99–295 MAD on Jumia. Your edge is **parapharmacie brand authority (`Kinédoux™`), kit presentation, service and trust**, not novelty.
3. **Imports are taxed from the first dirham.** Since 1 July 2022, e-commerce goods pay import duty (2.5%–40% depending on the HS code), 0.25% TPI and 20% VAT. Air freight DDP costs about 250 MAD/kg, so only very light items (under 150 g) can be flown in profitably. Anything bulky has to come by sea, or from a local wholesaler for the first test.

## 2. Chosen Niche & Brand Identity

### 2.1 Why this niche
- **Severe, daily, recognised pain.** Moroccan studies report chronic low-back pain at **32.6%** among hospital staff at CHU Hassan II in Fès, and **33.5%** among farmers in the El Jadida province (63.6% of cases triggered by lifting heavy loads). The same pattern shows up in taxi, truck and delivery drivers, office workers, cleaners, construction workers and mothers who carry children.
- **Proven local demand.** Several Moroccan stores already sell belts and neck devices. Demand exists, and the opening is a *specialist brand* rather than another multi-category gadget shop. Overview's 2026 Morocco data says the winners are stores with **small 2–3 product catalogs**, which is your model.
- **COD-friendly.** Light, unbreakable, one-size (or 2–3 sizes), easy to demonstrate on video, and no expiry date.
- **Gift dynamic.** "A gift for my father or mother" is a powerful Moroccan purchase motive. It raises AOV through second units and reduces refusals, because the buyer is emotionally committed.

## 3. Deep Dive: 3 Hero Products

### Landed-cost method (use this for every quote)
```
Landed cost (MAD) = FOB price × 9.85
                  + freight (sea LCL ≈ $105–135 per m³ Shenzhen→Casablanca; air DDP ≈ 250 MAD/kg)
                  + import duty (2.5% electronics → up to 40% textiles/finished goods)
                  + 0.25% TPI + 20% VAT on (value + freight + duty)
                  + forwarder/clearance fees
```
Ask **two forwarders for a DDP quote** (duties included) before ordering. Confirm the HS code and whether a conformity certificate is needed: electrical items can fall under Law 24-09 / CMIM conformity checks, and anything radio/Bluetooth needs ANRT approval. **Buy the non-Bluetooth versions.**

## 4. Ad Angles & Policy Compliance Guide

### 4.1 The rules that get Moroccan health-gadget accounts banned
| Rule (Meta Advertising Standards) | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Personal Attributes.** Ads can't assert or imply that the viewer has a medical condition or physical health issue | ❌ "واش كيضرك ظهرك؟" / "Vous souffrez de sciatique ?" ✅ "الجلسة الطويلة كتقل على الظهر" (a general statement) or a first-person testimonial |
| **Health & Wellness.** No statements of inferiority about appearance or body parts; health products must target 18+ | ❌ "Your posture looks old" ✅ "Stand tall with confidence" · Set age to **18+** (use 25–60) |
| **No before/after body transformations** | No side-by-side "hunched → straight" photos |
| **No medical / cure claims** (also required by Morocco's Law 84-12, since products presented as medical devices need AMMPS registration and their advertising is regulated) | ❌ علاج، كيداوي، الديسك، عرق النسا، hernie, guérit, "-70% douleur" ✅ راحة، تمدد خفيف، دعم، confort, soutien, détente |
| **Don't copy competitors' claims** | Competitors like Saneo and Miso claim "70% immediate pain reduction" or "treats herniated discs". Do not copy that copy—it puts your ad account and store at risk |

**Never** create multiple ad accounts to dodge bans. Use one verified Business Manager, verify **`kinedoux.com`** inside Meta Business Suite, and use a real, aged personal profile.

### 4.2 Banned vs. safe wording (Darija / French)
| ❌ Don't write | ✅ Write instead |

### 5.3 Phone call script (call within 15 minutes)

**Step 1: Opening (warm, short)**
> السلام عليكم، واش معايا السيد / السيدة [الاسم]؟
> أنا [الاسم] من **كينيدو (Kinédoux)**، كنعيط ليك على الطلب اللي درتي فالموقع ديالنا ديال حزام الظهر. عندك دقيقة وحدة؟

**Step 2: Light qualification (it makes the customer feel served, not sold to)**
> باش نتأكد بلي الحزام مناسب ليك: واش غادي تستعملو نتا، ولا هدية لشي حد؟
> شحال تقريبا القياس ديال الخصر؟ *(→ choose M or L / include extension strap)*
> واش كتستعملو أكثر فالخدمة، فالطوموبيل ولا فالدار؟

**Step 3: Restate the total (kills "surprise price" refusals)**
> الثمن هو **499 درهم**، التوصيل فابور، وكتخلص غير ملي توصلك الكولية فيدك. ماكاين حتى شي مصاريف أخرى.

**Step 4: Upsell (choose ONE, based on Step 2)**
- *Buying for self:*
> عندنا عرض خاص غير للكليان اللي كيأكدو فالتيليفون: تزيد **جهاز الكاسات الذكي Kinédoux Relax™** بـ **199 درهم** عوض 299، فنفس الكولية وبلا توصيل زايد. ممتاز لراحة الكتاف والظهر فالليل. نزيدهولك مع الحزام؟
- *Gift / family:*
> بزاف ديال الكليان كياخدو جوج: واحد ليهم وواحد للواليد ولا الواليدة. التاني كيجيك بـ **350 درهم** بلاصة 499، يعني جوج بـ **849 درهم**. بغيتي نزيدو؟

**Step 5: Exact address + GPS**
> عطيني العنوان كامل: المدينة، الحي، الزنقة، رقم الدار، وشي علامة قريبة باش الليفرور مايتلفش.
> غادي نصيفط ليك ميساج فالواتساب دابا، عافاك صيفط لينا اللوكاليزاسيون ديالك.

**Step 6: Availability (prevents "not home" RTS)**
> الكولية كتوصل فـ 24 حتى 48 ساعة. شمن نهار وشمن وقت تكون موجود؟
> واش الليفرور يعيط ليك فهاد الرقم، ولا عندك رقم آخر؟

**Step 7: Reassurance (state it once, calmly)**
> وباش تكون مرتاح: تقدر تشوف الكولية ديالك، وإلا كان شي عيب فالمنتوج ولا القياس ما جاش مزيان، كنبدلوه ليك فـ 14 يوم بلا مشاكل.

**Step 8: Closing + double confirmation**
> إذن نعاود معاك: حزام Kinédoux Air™، القياس [M]، **499 درهم**، التوصيل نهار [الخميس] لـ [الحي، المدينة]. صحيح؟
> دابا غادي نصيفط ليك ملخص الطلب فالواتساب، جاوبني غير بكلمة **"مؤكد"**. شكرا بزاف، وبالصحة مسبقا.

### 5.4 Objection handling

| Customer says | Answer (Darija) |
|---|---|
| **"غالي"** (too expensive) | فاهمك. هاد الحزام من لامارك **Kinédoux** ماشي ديال الجوطية: كيجي مع الحزام الإضافي ديال القياس، دليل الاستعمال بالدارجة والفرنسية، ضمان التبديل فـ 14 يوم، ومتابعة معانا فالواتساب. وكيخدم معاك سنين. وإلا بغيتي، نقدر نعطيك جوج بـ 849 ليك وللواليد. |
| **"غادي نفكر"** (I'll think about it) | ماكاين مشكل. غير باش نفهمك: شنو اللي باقي محيرك؟ الثمن، القياس، ولا واش غادي يناسبك؟ *(answer it)* … نقدر نخلي ليك الطلب محجوز حتى غدا، ونصيفط ليك فيديو قصير كيفاش كيتستعمل. |
| **"واش كيخدم بصح؟"** (does it really work?) | غادي نكون صريح معاك: ماشي دوا وما كنقولوش كيداوي. هو كيعطي دعم وتمدد خفيف للظهر ملي كتكون جالس ولا خدام بزاف، وبزاف ديال الكليان كيحسو بالراحة من الأيام الأولى، ولكن كل واحد وجسمو. وإلا عندك شي مشكل صحي كبير ولا عملية، شاور الطبيب ديالك قبل. |
| **"شكون انتوما؟ خايف"** (who are you? I'm worried) | حنا **Kinédoux.com**، ماركة متخصصة غير فراحة الظهر والوقفة فالمغرب. والخلاص عند الاستلام: ماكتخلص والو حتى توصلك الكولية وتشوفها. |
| **"ماعنديش الوقت دابا"** (no time now) | ماشي مشكل، شمن وقت نعاود نعيط ليك اليوم؟ *(book an exact time and call back on time)* |
| **"صيفطوه وصافي"** (just send it) | *(Still do Steps 3, 5 and 6 quickly. Skipping them causes RTS.)* |

### 5.5 Unreachable-customer protocol
1. Call at **T+15 min**, **T+3 h** and **T+evening (19:00–20:30)**.
2. Between calls, send WhatsApp text + a **15-second voice note** in Darija (much higher reply rate than text).
3. No answer after **48 h**: cancel. Never ship unconfirmed orders.

### 5.6 Pre-delivery messages (the RTS killers)
**Day of shipping:**
> السلام عليكم [الاسم]، الكولية ديالك من **Kinédoux** خرجات اليوم مع [شركة التوصيل]. الليفرور غادي يعيط ليك من رقم ماشي معروف، عافاك جاوبو. المبلغ: **499 درهم**. إلا كان شي تغيير فالعنوان ولا الوقت، قولها لينا دابا.

**Morning of delivery:**
> صباح الخير [الاسم]، الكولية ديال **Kinédoux** غادي توصلك اليوم إن شاء الله. وجد 499 درهم، وإلا ماكنتيش فالدار قولينا نبدلو الوقت.

**Day +2 after delivery (reviews + repeat):**
> كيف داير مع حزام Kinédoux Air™؟ ها فيديو ديال روتين 10 دقايق للظهر: [QR / link]. إلا عجبك، صيفط لينا تصويرة ولا رأي صغير، وعندك ناقص 15% على أي منتوج آخر فـ **kinedoux.com**.

### 5.7 Confirmation offer: what to offer and what NOT to offer
| Offer | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Free delivery + pay on delivery | ✅ Always. Show it everywhere |
| 14-day exchange (defect or wrong size) | ✅ Strong trust signal, low cost, and exceeds the statutory 7-day distance-selling baseline (Law 31-08) |
| 2nd unit for a parent at 350 MAD | ✅ Best AOV lever for Kit 1 |
| Phone-only upsell (+199 MAD Kit 3 *Kinédoux Relax™*) | ✅ Pure margin, same parcel, priced competitively against Jumia |
| "Buy 2 get 1 free" | ❌ On a 499 MAD item with 140 COGS it destroys margin and looks like a gimmick |
| "100% money back, no questions" | ⚠️ Only if you can actually process refunds. A broken promise brings complaints and bad comments |
| Allow opening the parcel before paying | ✅ Standard among top Moroccan competitors (Oknive, Roseelles, HPS Medical). Enable it with your courier and highlight it on the call |

## 6. Risk Management Guide ($500 budget)

### 6.1 Kill / scale rules (decide by numbers, not hope)
| Metric | Check after | Kill / fix if | Scale if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per order | 500 MAD spend per creative | 0 orders → kill the creative | — |
| Cost per order | 1,000 MAD total | > 120 MAD → change angle or offer | < 85 MAD |
| Confirmation rate | 15 orders | < 65% → check the form, price clarity, fake leads, reply speed | ≥ 80% |
| Delivery rate (of confirmed) | 10 shipped | < 70% → check the city mix, GPS collection, pre-delivery messages | ≥ 75% |
| **Scale** | 3 consecutive days in the green | — | +20–30% budget per day, never double overnight |

### 6.2 Red flags that burn small budgets
1. **Launching 3 products at once.** $190 split three ways teaches you nothing. One hero (*Kinédoux Air™*), one clean test.
2. **Buying 100+ units before validation.** Order samples first, then 10 local units, then a sea-freight batch of about 50–100 only after you have ≥15 delivered orders and a positive net.
3. **Health claims in ads or on `kinedoux.com`.** That risks a Meta account ban *and* Law 84-12 (products presented as medical devices need AMMPS registration, and their advertising is regulated). Stay on comfort and support language.
4. **Ingestible products** (teas, powders, supplements) without ONSSA/AMMPS authorization: illegal sale and seizure risk.
5. **Bluetooth gadgets** without ANRT approval, and electrical items without the required conformity documents: customs blocks.
6. **Air-freighting bulky items.** At ~250 MAD/kg DDP, a 0.7 kg belt costs ~175 MAD just to fly. Sea-freight or buy locally.
7. **Slow confirmation.** Every hour of delay costs about 10 points of conversion. If you can't reply within 15 minutes during ad hours, pause the ads during those hours.
8. **Shipping to rural zones in the test** (42% RTS vs 22–24% in Rabat and Casablanca).
9. **Bad timing.** Moroccan RTS peaks during **Ramadan (35%)**, **Aïd (38%)** and **Black Friday (32%)**; September is the lowest (22%). From today (late October 2026), launch now through mid-November or in December, not in Black Friday week. Don't start the test right before Ramadan 2027 (expected around early-to-mid February).
10. **Fake or duplicate orders.** Block repeated numbers, require the 10-digit 06/07 format, and ignore orders with joke names.
11. **Fake strike-through prices** ("599 → 199") and stock AliExpress photos. Moroccan buyers now recognise them instantly, and that sinks the "premium specialist" positioning.
12. **Ad account fragility.** Use one verified Business Manager with **`kinedoux.com`** domain verification and two admins. Don't use rented "agency accounts" from strangers.
13. **Cash-flow blindness.** Track every order in a sheet: ordered → confirmed → shipped → delivered → cash received. Choose a courier with fast remittance (Ameex advertises 24h, Cathedis weekly or twice weekly).
14. **No legal status.** Get an auto-entrepreneur registration (ICE number) before importing under your name or signing courier contracts. Verify the current requirements.

### 6.3 30-day timeline
| Days | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Connect **`kinedoux.com`** to your store & verify domain in Meta Business Suite; order Alibaba samples; get 3 local wholesale quotes for the belt (target ≤140 MAD); open courier accounts (Ameex or Cathedis + one backup) |
| 4–7 | Buy 10 Kit 1 units locally; film V1–V3 with real people; print **Kinédoux™** guide cards and stickers; build the landing page; check the Meta Ad Library links in §4.7 |
| 8–21 | Run the Meta test (130–150 MAD/day). Confirm every order within 15 minutes. Send pre-delivery messages. Log every metric |
| 15 | First read-out: kill or iterate creatives using §6.1 |
| 22–30 | If net is positive: request DDP sea quotes for 50 belts + 50 posture kits + 30 cupping devices; prepare Kit 2 creatives; switch on the Kit 3 upsell (+199 MAD) on every call |
